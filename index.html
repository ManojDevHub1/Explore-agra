Welcome to Agra

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is mainly known for the Taj Mahal, one of the most famous monuments in the world. The city also has forts, gardens, old markets and many places connected with Indian history.

Agra can be a good place to visit for people who enjoy history, architecture and traditional Indian food. A short trip can include famous monuments as well as local markets and restaurants.

Why Visit Agra?

Agra is a city where you can see important Mughal-era buildings and learn about the history of India. The Taj Mahal and Agra Fort are two of its best-known places. Visitors can also try local foods and explore traditional markets.

Best Time to Visit

The cooler months from October to March are generally more comfortable for sightseeing. Summer can be very hot, so visitors should plan outdoor activities carefully during the hottest part of the day.

Places to Visit in Agra

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a famous white marble monument built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan. It is located on the bank of the Yamuna River and is known for its detailed architecture and beautiful gardens.

Location: Dharmapuri, Agra

Suggested Visit Duration: 2 to 3 hours

Learn More About Taj Mahal

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a large historic fort made mainly of red sandstone. It contains palaces, halls and other buildings that show the history of the Mughal period.

Location: Rakabganj, Agra

Suggested Visit Duration: 1.5 to 2 hours

Learn More About Agra Fort

Mehtab Bagh

Mehtab Bagh is a garden located across the Yamuna River from the Taj Mahal. It is a peaceful place and is known for views of the Taj Mahal, especially around sunset.

Location: Near the Yamuna River, Agra

Suggested Visit Duration: 1 hour

Learn More About Mehtab Bagh

Itmad-ud-Daulah

The Tomb of Itmad-ud-Daulah is a beautiful marble monument in Agra. It is sometimes called the "Baby Taj" because of its size and detailed decoration. The monument is surrounded by a garden.

Location: Moti Bagh, Agra

Suggested Visit Duration: 1 hour

Learn More About Itmad-ud-Daulah

Fatehpur Sikri

Fatehpur Sikri is a historic city located near Agra. It was built during the reign of Emperor Akbar and contains palaces, courtyards, mosques and other historic buildings.

Location: Fatehpur Sikri, Uttar Pradesh

Suggested Visit Duration: 2 to 3 hours

Learn More About Fatehpur Sikri

Famous Food in Agra

  • Agra Petha: A soft and sweet dessert made from ash gourd. It is one of the city's most well-known local foods.
  • Mughlai Food: Agra has many restaurants serving Mughlai dishes such as kebabs, biryani, korma and various gravies.
  • Bedai: A popular breakfast dish made with a crispy fried bread, usually served with spicy potato curry and sometimes sweets.
  • Dalmoth: A crunchy and spicy snack made with lentils, nuts and spices. It is also commonly taken home as a local snack.

Travel Tips for Agra

Things to Remember

  • Carry a water bottle during sightseeing.
  • Wear comfortable shoes because some places require a lot of walking.
  • Carry some cash for small shops and local food stalls.
  • Keep your important documents and belongings safe.
  • Check opening days and visiting hours before going to a monument.

Simple Day Plan

  1. Start the day with a visit to the Taj Mahal.
  2. Have breakfast or tea at a local restaurant.
  3. Visit Agra Fort.
  4. Explore a local market and try Agra's famous food.
  5. Visit Mehtab Bagh in the evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Agra famous for?

Agra is most famous for the Taj Mahal. It is also known for Agra Fort, Mughal history and local foods such as petha.


How many days are enough for Agra?

One or two days can be enough to visit the main attractions in Agra. People who want to explore more places can stay longer.


What food should I try in Agra?

You can try Agra Petha, Mughlai food, Bedai and Dalmoth during your visit.


Which season is comfortable for visiting Agra?

October to March is generally more comfortable for sightseeing because the weather is cooler than the summer months.


Is Agra suitable for a short trip?

Yes. Many of the famous attractions are located within and around the city, so the main sights can be visited during a short trip.
